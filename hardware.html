Hardware Guide

Overview of available Grove sensors and what they measure.

Grove - Light Sensor v1.2

Measures the intensity of ambient light to detect brightness.

Grove - Sound Sensor

Detects the sound loudness of the environment in percentages.

Grove - Temperature Sensor (Analog)

Measures the ambient temperature using a thermistor.

Grove - Rotary Angle Sensor

A potentiometer that generates a value based on its rotational position.

Grove - Push Button

A digital push button that outputs PRESSED or IDLE states.

Grove - Ultrasonic Distance Sensor

Measures the distance to an object in centimeters using sound waves.

Grove - Temp & Humidity Sensor v1.2

A digital DHT11 sensor capturing both Celsius temperature and relative humidity.

Grove - BMI088 (6-Axis)

An I2C aerospace grade sensor capturing 3-axis acceleration and gyroscope data.

Grove - MMA7660 (3-Axis)

An I2C accelerometer tracking movement and orientation in three dimensions.
